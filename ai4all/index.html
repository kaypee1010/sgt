The three tiers

  1. Tier 1: AI LiteracyAI Foundations for every student: how AI and data work, prompting, verification, responsible use. 2 credits, mandatory.
  2. Tier 2: Applied AIEach School's own course on how AI is used in its profession, and where it must stop. 2 credits, mandatory.
  3. Tier 3: Core AI/MLPython and machine learning with AI-assisted coding, for technical tracks and an AI minor. Optional.
