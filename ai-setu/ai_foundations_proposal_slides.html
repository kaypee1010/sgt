Section 1

Rationale and Context

1.1 Academic

  • UGC Curriculum and Credit Framework lists AI and ML among Value-Added Courses
  • Aligned with the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students (2024)

1.2 Industry

  • Documentation, analysis, diagnostics, legal research and media now run with AI in the loop
  • Employers expect AI use without loss of judgement
  • India's AI Governance Guidelines and ICMR guidelines keep the human accountable

1.3 Student

  • Unguided use can weaken independent performance (Bastani et al., PNAS)
  • AI tools fabricate references (Walters and Wilder, Scientific Reports)
  • Indian schools begin teaching AI from 2026-27

1.4 Institutional

  • Strengthens NAAC and NIRF positioning
  • Prepares for data-protection obligations from May 2027
  • A shared base for interdisciplinary AI research

Section 3

Programme Objectives and Student Benefit

Programme objectives

  • A correct, non-technical understanding of predictive and generative AI
  • Specify tasks to AI through clear, structured, iterative prompts
  • Make verification of claims and citations a routine, assessed habit
  • Judgement on privacy, confidentiality, copyright, integrity and disclosure
  • Design human-checked AI workflows for real tasks in their own field

What the student gets

  • A certified, resume-ready AI credential alongside the core degree
  • Hands-on practice, not theoretical awareness
  • A second, domain-specific credit tied to their own discipline
  • Improved employability: recruiters now screen for basic AI fluency
  • No cost in Year 1

Section 5

Curriculum Architecture: Three Tiers

This proposal

Tier 1 · AI Foundations (2 credits)

Who: every student, 19 non-engineering Schools, compulsory

How AI and language models work, prompting, verification, bias and hallucination checks, ethics and responsible use.

This proposal

Tier 2 · Applied AI / Future Track (1 credit)

Who: every student, inside their own School

Domain-specific application using pre-built and no-code tools, plus the professional boundaries of AI in that field.

Outside scope

Who: SOET and technical tracks only

Python, regression, classification and model building, already inside SOET's curriculum.

Section 9

Industry Alignment

Near term

Cluster mapping validated with each School's Dean and HOD during Year 1.

Medium term

Structured input from industry advisors per cluster: legal-tech, health-tech, creative AI.

Longer term

A formal collaboration with a partner such as Microsoft or Xebia.

Microsoft

Microsoft Learn pathways (Azure AI Fundamentals, AI Business Professional), Generative AI for Beginners. SGT would seek: co-branded or recognised certification, curriculum alignment, guest sessions and cloud credits.

Xebia

Data and AI training, role-based upskilling, Microsoft Solutions Partner for training. SGT would seek: enterprise benchmarking, a certified trainer network, applied projects and internships.

Nothing in this proposal commits the university; neither partnership affects the Year 1 budget or team size.
